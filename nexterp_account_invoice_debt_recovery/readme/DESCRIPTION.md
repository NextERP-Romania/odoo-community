Follow overdue customer invoices that were handed over for debt recovery,
right on the invoice. The invoice is clearly marked, keeps its own payment
status until it is settled, and carries the details of the case.

- **Mark as Debt Recovery** button on unpaid or partly paid customer invoices.
- A *Debt Recovery* ribbon and payment status on the invoice until it is paid.
- **Debt Recovery** tab with status (*Notification* or *Lawyer*), case date,
  law firm, amount in litigation, commission, penalties and notes.
- **Debt Recovery** filter and **Payment Status** grouping on the invoice
  list, optional **Debt Status** and **Case Date** columns.
- Payments can still be registered; the case is marked done once the invoice
  is fully paid.
- Available for companies located in Romania, enabled per company.
