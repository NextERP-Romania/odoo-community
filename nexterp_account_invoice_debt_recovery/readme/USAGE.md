## Send an invoice to debt recovery

1. Open an unpaid or partly paid customer invoice from
   **Invoicing > Customers > Invoices**.
2. Click **Mark as Debt Recovery**. The invoice gets the *Debt Recovery*
   ribbon and payment status.
3. Open the **Debt Recovery** tab and fill in **Debt Status**, **Case Date**,
   **Law Firm**, **Amount in Litigation**, **Commission (%)**,
   **Penalties (%)** and the notes.
4. Save.

## Follow the cases

1. In **Invoicing > Customers > Invoices**, use the **Debt Recovery** filter.
2. Group by **Payment Status**, or show the **Debt Status** and **Case Date**
   columns from the optional columns menu.

## Close a case

1. When money comes in, click **Pay** on the invoice and register the payment.
2. Once the invoice is fully paid, **Debt Recovery Done** is ticked on the
   **Debt Recovery** tab automatically.
