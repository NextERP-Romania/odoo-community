Analytic distribution models fill in the analytic distribution of journal
items automatically, based on the account, partner, product and so on. This
module adds the **Journal** as one more criterion, so a model can apply only
to what is posted in one journal.

- A **Journal** field on every analytic distribution model, in the form and as
  a column in the list.
- A model with a journal applies only to items posted in that journal; items
  from other journals ignore it.
- Models without a journal keep applying everywhere, as before.
- Useful to split costs differently per sales channel, purchase journal, bank
  or miscellaneous journal without extra rules.
