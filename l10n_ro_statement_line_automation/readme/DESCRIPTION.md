Bank transactions imported or received one by one are gathered
automatically into one bank statement per journal and per day, for Romanian
companies. Nobody has to open a daily statement before the lines come in.

- **One statement per day**: each new bank line joins the statement of its
  journal and date, or a new one is opened for it.
- **Running balance kept up to date**: the ending balance of a statement
  opened this way follows the lines that join it.
- **Your statements respected**: a complete statement you entered yourself
  is never extended, and its ending balance is never rewritten; an
  incomplete one of the same day is filled in.
- **Works with any source**: bank file imports, online bank feeds or
  integrations that create bank lines.
- **Romanian companies only**: other companies of the same database keep
  the standard behaviour.
