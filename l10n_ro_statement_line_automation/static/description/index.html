Overview

Bank transactions imported or received one by one are gathered automatically into one bank statement per journal and per day, for Romanian companies. Nobody has to open a daily statement before the lines come in.

  • One statement per day: each new bank line joins the statement of its journal and date, or a new one is opened for it.
  • Running balance kept up to date: the ending balance of a statement opened this way follows the lines that join it.
  • Your statements respected: a complete statement you entered yourself is never extended, and its ending balance is never rewritten; an incomplete one of the same day is filled in.
  • Works with any source: bank file imports, online bank feeds or integrations that create bank lines.
  • Romanian companies only: other companies of the same database keep the standard behaviour.

Configuration

Nothing to configure. Make sure that:

  1. the company's Country is Romania (Settings > Users & Companies > Companies);
  2. the bank journals exist in Invoicing > Configuration > Accounting > Journals.

From then on, every bank line created without a statement is placed in a daily statement.

How it works

  1. Import a bank file, receive transactions from a bank feed, or add lines on a bank journal.
  2. Open the bank journal from the Invoicing dashboard: each line already sits in the statement of its day, with the balance computed.
  3. Reconcile the lines as usual. The module only places them in statements; matching, amounts, partners and labels are left as they came.
