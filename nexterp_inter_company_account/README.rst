Flags invoices, journal entries and journal items made with another
company of the group, so they can be shown alone or left out of any
accounting list or analysis.

-  Every invoice and journal entry whose partner is a group company is
   flagged inter-company; its journal items follow.
-  **Inter Company** and **Without Inter Company** filters on invoices,
   bills, journal entries, journal items and **Invoice Analysis**.
-  Existing documents are flagged at installation, even on large
   databases.
-  Group companies come from ``nexterp_inter_company``.

Configuration
=============

1. Install ``nexterp_inter_company``: the partners of the companies of
   the database are marked as inter-company automatically.
2. For group companies kept in another database, open their contact and
   tick **Is Inter Company**.
3. Install this module. Existing documents are flagged during
   installation; no other setting is needed.

Usage
=====

1. Open **Invoicing > Customers > Invoices** or **Invoicing > Vendors >
   Bills**.
2. In the search options choose **Inter Company** to see only documents
   with group companies, or **Without Inter Company** to leave them out.
3. The same filters are available in **Invoicing > Accounting > Journal
   Entries**, **Invoicing > Accounting > Journal Items** and **Invoicing
   > Reporting > Invoice Analysis**, for example to read sales to third
   parties only.
