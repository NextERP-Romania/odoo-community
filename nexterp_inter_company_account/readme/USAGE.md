1. Open **Invoicing > Customers > Invoices** or **Invoicing > Vendors >
   Bills**.
2. In the search options choose **Inter Company** to see only documents with
   group companies, or **Without Inter Company** to leave them out.
3. The same filters are available in **Invoicing > Accounting > Journal
   Entries**, **Invoicing > Accounting > Journal Items** and
   **Invoicing > Reporting > Invoice Analysis**, for example to read sales
   to third parties only.
