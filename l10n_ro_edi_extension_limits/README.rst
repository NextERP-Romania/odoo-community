Keeps e-Factura invoices within the maximum text lengths ANAF accepts,
so an invoice is not refused because a name, an address or a note is too
long.

-  **Automatic shortening**: partner names and addresses, references,
   payment details, discount and charge reasons, VAT exemption reasons
   and product names and descriptions are cut to the lengths the
   Romanian e-invoicing rules allow.
-  **Notes kept in full**: long invoice and line notes are not cut but
   split into several notes of the allowed size, so no text is lost.
-  **Counted like ANAF counts**: lengths are measured in characters, the
   way the ANAF validator measures them, diacritics included.
-  **Nothing to set up**: installed together with
   ``l10n_ro_edi_extension``.

Configuration
=============

Nothing to configure. The module is installed automatically with
``l10n_ro_edi_extension`` and applies to every e-Factura invoice from
then on.

Usage
=====

1. Create and confirm a customer invoice as usual, with names,
   descriptions or notes as long as you need.
2. Send it to ANAF (**Send & Print**, **Send to ANAF** or the scheduled
   sending).
3. The XML that leaves Odoo keeps every text within the allowed length:
   long notes are split into several notes, other fields are shortened.
   The invoice in Odoo and its PDF are not changed.
