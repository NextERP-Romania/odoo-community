1. Open a quotation in **Sales > Orders > Quotations** that already has
   at least one line.
2. Change the **Pricelist**.
3. The unit prices and discounts of the lines are recomputed at once from
   the new pricelist.
4. If the quotation was already saved, a message in the chatter records
   which pricelist the prices were taken from.
5. Save the quotation.

To go back to updating prices by hand, untick **Auto Update Sales Prices**
in the settings: the next pricelist change shows the **Update Prices**
button again.
