Overview

Run physical inventories as documents: choose the locations and products, generate the count sheet from current stock, enter what was counted and validate. Every adjustment is booked on the date you choose and kept in a history you can report on.

  • Inventory documents per date, locations and products.
  • Count lines generated from the stock on hand, with quantities and values.
  • Validation books the differences in stock and accounting on the chosen date.
  • Adjustments made elsewhere are captured in inventory documents too.
  • A validated inventory is locked.
  • Inventory Stock Line Adjustments History report with list, pivot and graph views.

Configuration

  1. Give the people running inventories the Inventory / Administrator access right in Settings > Users & Companies > Users; the menus are visible to this group.
  2. Make sure the locations to count are internal locations (Inventory > Configuration > Warehouse Management > Locations).
  3. Stock valuation must be active (the module relies on stock accounting).

How it works

  1. Go to Inventory > Operations > Adjustments > Inventory Stock Adjustments and click New.
  2. Set the Accounting Date, the locations and, optionally, the products.
  3. Click Generate Inventory Lines to list the stock on hand.
  4. Enter the counted quantity on each line; add lines for goods found but not in stock. Use Clear Inventory Lines to start over.
  5. Click Validate Inventory: the differences are booked and the document is set to Done.
  6. Review all counted lines in Inventory > Reporting > Inventory Stock Line Adjustments History.

Versions

19.0.1.1.1 (2026-11-28)

  • Apps listing: English description, configuration and usage, new icon and cover.

19.0.1.1.0 (2026-11-05)

  • A validated inventory is frozen: it cannot be validated again, regenerated, cleared, edited or deleted, and neither can its lines. A draft one deletes with its lines.
  • Validating applies the counted quantities directly, also when goods moved after the count, and marks the document done only once the stock was adjusted. Before, Odoo's conflict wizard was returned and ignored: nothing was applied, yet the inventory was set to done.
  • An adjustment made on the quants that ends in Odoo's conflict wizard no longer leaves a done inventory behind; the document is made when the conflict is settled.
  • Lines keep their unit of measure after the quant they counted is cleaned up; their location, product and lot cannot be changed once the quant is set; goods tracked by lot cannot be counted in without one.
  • The company of the inventory and the inventory of a line are now required (lines are deleted with their draft inventory); orphan lines are dropped on upgrade.
