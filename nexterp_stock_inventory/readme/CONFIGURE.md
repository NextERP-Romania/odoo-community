1. Give the people running inventories the **Inventory / Administrator**
   access right in **Settings > Users & Companies > Users**; the menus are
   visible to this group.
2. Make sure the locations to count are internal locations
   (**Inventory > Configuration > Warehouse Management > Locations**).
3. Stock valuation must be active (the module relies on stock accounting).

For the printed report, list the members of the commission on the
**Inventory Commission** tab of each inventory and fill in the decision
number in the **Inventory Report** group.
