Overview

Completes eTransport, the ANAF notification for the transport of goods with high fiscal risk, so that notifications are filled in correctly and can be followed through their whole life from the transfer.

Correct data in the notification

  • Values without VAT taken from the right source: cost on receipts, sale price on deliveries, or a source you choose per company or per transfer (cost, purchase order, sale order or sales price).
  • Quantities, units, weights and customs tariff codes computed consistently; the gross weight is never below the net weight.
  • Addresses split into street and number the way ANAF expects.

More of what ANAF allows

  • Several transport documents per notification: CMR, invoice, delivery note or other.
  • Previous notifications for the operations that need them.
  • Post-outage declaration for notifications sent after an ANAF outage.

The whole life of a notification

  • Delete, Confirm transport (confirmed, partially confirmed or refused) and Modify vehicle, sent to ANAF from the transfer; each waits for ANAF's answer before it counts.
  • Notifications list: the notifications ANAF holds for the company, matched with the transfers, by hand or every six hours.
  • Transporters: the notifications in which the company is the carrier, with vehicles and routes.
  • Protected transfers: a transfer whose notification is alive at ANAF cannot be deleted or cancelled, and its declared data are frozen while ANAF processes it.

Configuration

  1. Set up eTransport and the ANAF connection as required by the Romanian eTransport localization.
  2. Go to Invoicing > Configuration > Settings, section Romanian E-Factura, block eTransport additional settings:
    • Default eTransport Price Source: where the values without VAT come from; Automatic (by operation) suits most companies;
    • Automatic List Sync: tick it to match the ANAF notifications list with the transfers every six hours;
    • List Sync Days: how many days back each synchronization looks (1 to 60).
  3. Make sure every product moved under eTransport has its customs tariff code (4, 6 or 8 digits) on the product form.
  4. Write partner addresses as street name and number (for instance Calea Victoriei 12-14), so the street and the number are split correctly.
  5. Optionally, in developer mode, activate the scheduled action eTransport: fetch ANAF status of transfers in Settings > Technical > Automation > Scheduled Actions, to fetch the answers to deletions, confirmations and vehicle changes automatically.

How it works

Sending a notification

  1. Open the transfer in Inventory > Operations > Transfers and go to the eTransport tab.
  2. Check eTransport Price Source, and tick Post-Outage Declaration if the notification is sent after an ANAF outage.
  3. Under Transport documents, add the CMR, invoice, delivery note or other documents (for Other, the remarks are required).
  4. For the operations that need them, fill in the previous notifications.
  5. Click Check eTransport to see any error before sending, then click Send eTransport. The UIT received from ANAF is stored on the transfer.

After the UIT is received

  1. Use the buttons of the transfer: Delete notification, Confirm transport (choose confirmed, partially confirmed or refused) or Modify vehicle (new vehicle and trailers, date and time).
  2. The action waits as Action Sent until ANAF's answer is fetched (Fetch Status, or the scheduled action). Accepted, it is recorded; refused, the error is shown and the action can be sent again.
  3. All the documents exchanged with ANAF are listed in Inventory > eTransport > Documents.

Checking what ANAF holds

  1. Inventory > eTransport > Notifications list: enter the number of days and click Query ANAF; the notifications found are matched with the transfers and noted in their chatter.
  2. Inventory > eTransport > Transporters: enter the carrier's VAT number (and optionally a UIT or reference) and click Query ANAF to see the notifications in which the company is the carrier.

Versions

19.0.1.8.4

  • Apps listing: English description, configuration and usage, new icon and cover.

19.0.1.3.1

  • The parser that locates the element behind an ANAF error no longer resolves entities and cannot reach the network.

19.0.1.3.0 (2026-11-05)

  • A transfer whose UIT is alive at ANAF (sent, validated, confirmed or with a changed vehicle) can no longer be deleted or cancelled; the deletion (DEL) has to be sent first. A failed correction does not release it, since ANAF still holds the validated notification.
  • The deletion, confirmation and vehicle change are refused on the server unless ANAF validated the notification (a vehicle change keeps it so) and the UIT is the transfer's current one.
  • After a vehicle change the Delete, Confirm, Modify vehicle and Amend buttons stay available.
  • While ANAF processes a notification, the declared eTransport data of the transfer, its transport documents and its previous notifications are frozen on the server too, not only in the form.
  • eTransport documents cannot be deleted from their form either.
  • A deletion, confirmation or vehicle change now waits in the new "Action Sent" state until its processing status is fetched from ANAF (Fetch Status button, or the new, inactive by default, "eTransport: fetch ANAF status of transfers" scheduled action), instead of being recorded as done as soon as it was uploaded. Accepted: the document moves to Deleted / Confirmed / Vehicle Modified (the new vehicle is written on the transfer only then). Refused: it moves to Error with ANAF's message and the UIT stays where it was, so the transfer stays guarded and the action can be sent again. A pending deletion still holds the transfer.
  • Uninstalling the module keeps the eTransport documents of these actions: confirmations and vehicle changes become Validated (the UIT is still valid), deletions and pending actions become Error with their original state noted in the message. Before, they were deleted.

19.0.1.2.0 (2026-09-15)

  • Notifications for intra-EU, import and export operations can be sent again: the check that the counterparty's warehouse is in Romania no longer blocks them.
  • An unexpected answer from ANAF is reported as a clear error instead of stopping the operation.
  • A transfer can be sent to eTransport in every state where it makes sense, not only in the states the base localization allowed.

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-06-23)
