## Sending a notification

1. Open the transfer in **Inventory > Operations > Transfers** and go to
   the **eTransport** tab.
2. Check **eTransport Price Source**, and tick **Post-Outage Declaration**
   if the notification is sent after an ANAF outage.
3. Under **Transport documents**, add the CMR, invoice, delivery note or
   other documents (for *Other*, the remarks are required).
4. For the operations that need them, fill in the previous notifications.
5. Click **Check eTransport** to see any error before sending, then click
   **Send eTransport**. The UIT received from ANAF is stored on the
   transfer.

## After the UIT is received

1. Use the buttons of the transfer: **Delete notification**, **Confirm
   transport** (choose confirmed, partially confirmed or refused) or
   **Modify vehicle** (new vehicle and trailers, date and time).
2. The action waits as *Action Sent* until ANAF's answer is fetched
   (**Fetch Status**, or the scheduled action). Accepted, it is recorded;
   refused, the error is shown and the action can be sent again.
3. All the documents exchanged with ANAF are listed in **Inventory >
   eTransport > Documents**.

## Checking what ANAF holds

1. **Inventory > eTransport > Notifications list**: enter the number of
   days and click **Query ANAF**; the notifications found are matched with
   the transfers and noted in their chatter.
2. **Inventory > eTransport > Transporters**: enter the carrier's VAT
   number (and optionally a UIT or reference) and click **Query ANAF** to
   see the notifications in which the company is the carrier.
