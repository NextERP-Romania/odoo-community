Romania - E-invoicing Storno Credit Notes

Sends customer credit notes (out_refund) to ANAF e-Factura / SPV as a storno invoice: UBL Invoice with InvoiceTypeCode 380 and negative values, instead of a CreditNote 381 with positive values.

What this module provides

  • Company setting l10n_ro_credit_note_einvoice (“Send Credit Notes as Invoice 380 with negative values”). The behaviour applies only to companies where it is enabled; all others keep the standard 381 credit note.
  • Invoice 380 instead of CreditNote 381 — root <Invoice>, cac:InvoiceLine and cbc:InvoicedQuantity.
  • Negative quantities and amounts — lines, allowances/charges, VAT totals and all LegalMonetaryTotal amounts. The item net price (BT-146) stays positive (BR-27).
  • Reference to the original invoice — BT-25 (number) and BT-26 (issue date) in cac:BillingReference, taken from the reversed invoice.
  • No DocumentTypeCode 50 on the embedded PDF, since that code is only allowed on a CreditNote (UBL-SR-43).
  • Printed PDF as a storno invoice — “Invoice” title and “Invoice Date” instead of “Credit Note”, a “Reversal of invoice … dated …” line, negative quantities, line amounts, taxes, totals, payments and amount due; the unit price stays positive.

Configuration

Configuration

  1. Go to Settings → Accounting, section Romanian E-Factura.
  2. Enable Send Credit Notes as Invoice 380 with negative values.

The setting is per company. Accounting entries are not changed: the credit note stays an out_refund in Odoo, only the XML sent to ANAF changes.
