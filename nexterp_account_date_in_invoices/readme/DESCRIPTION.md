For companies with Romania as fiscal country, the accounting date of a
vendor bill follows the bill date, so the expense and the deductible VAT land
in the month written on the supplier's invoice.

- The **Accounting Date** of vendor bills and purchase receipts is set to the
  **Bill Date**.
- If the bill date falls in a period already closed by the tax lock date, the
  accounting date moves to the first day after the lock date, so a closed VAT
  return is never changed.
- Customer invoices and companies of other countries keep the standard
  behaviour.
- Nothing to configure.
