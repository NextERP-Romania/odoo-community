1. Open a confirmed invoice from **Invoicing > Customers > Invoices**.
2. Click **Print**: the PDF shows the **VAT** and **Total** columns
   enabled in the settings, with quantities printed at the precision of their
   unit.
3. Open a credit note from **Invoicing > Customers > Credit Notes** and print
   it: with **Print Show Refunds** on, quantities, amounts, taxes and totals
   are printed with a minus sign.
