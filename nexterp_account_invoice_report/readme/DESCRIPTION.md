Adds options to the printed invoice that accountants often ask for: VAT and
total per line, credit notes printed with negative amounts, and a print
precision per unit of measure.

- **VAT** column with the tax amount of each line, also on section subtotals.
- **Total** column with the amount including taxes of each line.
- Credit notes printed with negative quantities, amounts, taxes and totals,
  as a storno document.
- **Report Precision** on each unit of measure: how many decimals the printed
  quantity shows (for example three for kilograms, none for pieces).
- All options are per company and work on detailed and grouped invoice
  layouts.
