1. Open a kit product: its **Sales Price** and **Cost** show the sum of its
   components.
2. Change a component's price or quantity; the kit price follows.
3. Use the kit on quotations: with a pricelist, each component is priced by
   the pricelist and the results are added up.
4. Go to **Sales > Products > Product Kits** to review every kit line,
   grouped by category, kit and component.

To show the components on sales orders install `nexterp_product_kit_sale`.
