Sell bundles made of other products and let their price follow the
components. A kit lists its components with quantities; its sales price and
cost are the sum of the components, and pricelists are applied component by
component.

- **Kit Products** tab on the product and on each variant.
- **Is a Kit Component** option that keeps components out of the regular
  sales catalog.
- Sales price and cost computed from the components, always up to date.
- Pricelist rules applied on each component, so promotions on a component
  reach the kit.
- **Product Kits** report with list and pivot views of every kit line.
- Safeguards: quantities must be positive and a kit cannot contain itself.
