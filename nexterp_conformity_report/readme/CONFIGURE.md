1. Go to **Settings > Users & Companies > Companies**, open your company and
   check that its name, street and city are filled in: they are printed in
   the declaration.
2. Fill in the VAT and trade register number on your customers so they
   appear under the customer address.
3. For the **Lot** and **Minimum shelf life** columns, track the products by
   lots and set an expiration date on the lots.

The delegate and the means of transport come from the Romanian delivery
fields provided by `l10n_ro_stock_picking_comment_template`.
