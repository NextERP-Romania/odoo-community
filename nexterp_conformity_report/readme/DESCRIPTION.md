Print a *Certificate of Conformity* for every delivery, ready to be signed
when the goods are handed over.

- Available from the **Print** menu of any done delivery.
- Customer details, delivery number and date, sales order and customer
  reference in the header.
- One row per delivered line: product, customer product code, lot, unit of
  measure, quantity and minimum shelf life (lot expiration date).
- A conformity declaration issued by your company, followed by signature
  blocks for the agent and the delegate, with the means of transport.
- Printed in the language of your company.
