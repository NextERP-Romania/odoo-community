With the Romanian e-Factura integration, every invoice of a Romanian company
is a candidate for sending to the national e-invoicing system (SPV). This
module lets you choose, journal by journal, which invoices go there, so
internal, test or out-of-scope journals never upload anything.

- **Enable EDI Send** option on the journal, shown on sales journals and on
  purchase journals marked **Self Billing**.
- Invoices of a journal without the option are sent and printed normally,
  without the e-Factura upload.
- Every journal must be switched on explicitly: nothing is sent by surprise.
- Documents already sent for past invoices are kept as they are.
