1. Create and confirm an invoice in a journal with **Enable EDI Send**
   ticked.
2. Click **Send**: the e-Factura option is offered as usual and the invoice is
   uploaded to SPV.
3. Create and confirm an invoice in a journal without the option.
4. Click **Send**: no e-Factura option is offered; the invoice is only
   emailed or printed.

The option can be changed at any time on the journal; it applies to the next
invoices sent from that journal.
