Nothing to configure: the control starts with the first session closed
after installation.

- **Point of Sale / User** can fill in the causes and the explanatory
  note; **Point of Sale / Administrator** can also delete control lines.
- Only payment methods of type *Cash* or *Bank* are controlled; *Customer
  Account* methods are left out. Several bank methods on one register
  (card terminal, meal vouchers) are controlled separately.
