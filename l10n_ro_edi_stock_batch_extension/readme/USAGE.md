1. Open the batch in **Inventory > Operations > Jobs > Batch Transfers**
   and go to the **eTransport** tab.
2. Check **eTransport Price Source**, add the **Transport documents**, the
   previous notifications if the operation needs them, and tick
   **Post-Outage Declaration** if needed.
3. Click **Check eTransport**, then click **Send eTransport**. The UIT is
   stored on the batch.
4. Later, use **Delete notification**, **Confirm transport** or **Modify
   vehicle** on the batch; ANAF's answer is fetched with **Fetch Status**
   or by the scheduled action and noted in the chatter.
