1. Open the invoice to remove, for example from
   **Invoicing > Customers > Invoices**. It must be the last invoice numbered
   in its journal.
2. Click **Reset to Draft**.
3. Delete it with **Actions > Delete** (form) or from the list view.
4. Create the next invoice in the same journal: it receives the number the
   deleted invoice had, so no gap is left.

If the invoice is not the last one of its journal, it is deleted the standard
way and its number is not reused.
