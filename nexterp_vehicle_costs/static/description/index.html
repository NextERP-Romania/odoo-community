Overview

Fuel, parts and services for company vehicles are bought on vendor bills and taken out of the warehouse, but the fleet only knows about the costs typed in by hand. This module books the vehicle costs straight from the bills and from the stock issued to the vehicles.

  • Vehicle Service Type on vendor bill lines: posting the bill records the cost on the vehicle, with product, quantity and unit price.
  • Vehicle on stock moves: goods issued to a consumption location for a vehicle become a vehicle cost when the transfer is validated.
  • Goods returned from the vehicle book a negative cost that reverses the original one; cancelling a bill removes the costs it created.
  • Costs booked from stock follow their move and cannot be edited or deleted by hand.
  • Owner on the vehicle, carried to every service and contract cost.
  • Romanian non-deductible VAT per vehicle: a percentage and a tax applied to the goods it consumes.
  • Costs Log report, in pivot and list, per vehicle, type and date.
  • Fuel and parts service type categories, with a ready-made refuelling service type.

Configuration

Vehicles

  1. Go to Fleet > Fleet > Fleet and open a vehicle.
  2. Set the Owner of the vehicle.
  3. If VAT on what the vehicle consumes is not fully deductible, tick Not Deductible, choose the Non Deductible Percent and the non-deductible tax to apply.
  4. Save.

Service types

  1. Activate the developer mode, then go to Fleet > Configuration > Services > Types (Service Types on newer versions).
  2. Check the Category of each type: Combustibil (fuel), Parts, Service or Contract. Bill lines and stock moves only offer the non-contract types.
  3. A refuelling service type with the Combustibil (fuel) category is installed with the module.

Consumption location

Stock is booked as a vehicle cost when it is moved to a location of type consumption. Make sure the location the vehicles take their goods to has that type, in Inventory > Configuration > Locations.

How it works

Costs from vendor bills

  1. Go to Accounting > Vendors > Bills and create a bill for fuel, parts or a service.
  2. On each line that concerns a vehicle, choose the Vehicle and the Vehicle Service Type.
  3. Post the bill. The cost appears on the vehicle, under its services.
  4. If the bill is cancelled, the costs it created are removed; posting it again creates them anew.

Costs from stock

  1. Create an internal transfer or a consumption from the warehouse to the vehicles' consumption location.
  2. On the move, choose the Vehicle and, if needed, the Vehicle Service Type.
  3. Validate the transfer. The value of the goods is booked as a cost of the vehicle; for a non-deductible vehicle, its percentage and tax are applied.
  4. To correct it, return the goods: the return books a negative cost on the same vehicle.

Reporting

  1. Go to Fleet > Reporting > Costs Log.
  2. Filter by vehicle, service type or period and switch between the pivot and list views to compare costs per vehicle and per category.

19.0.1.1.0 (2026-11-05)

  • A vehicle cost booked from a done stock move follows the move: its amount, vehicle, product and quantities cannot be edited and it cannot be deleted; notes stay editable. Costs typed by hand are not affected.
  • The vehicle (or the refuelling flag) of a done move that already has a cost cannot be changed any more.
  • Returning goods from the vehicle's consumption location books a negative cost on the same vehicle, reversing the original one.
  • Contract-type service categories are recognised (the comparison used "Contract" instead of "contract").
  • Uninstalling the module turns the fuel and parts service types into plain services instead of deleting them together with their costs.
  • Cancelling a vendor bill removes the vehicle service costs made from its lines when it was posted (posting it again makes them anew).
  • Removed code that could never run: a stock move cancellation override (there is no such method), a bill-line cost cancellation on fields that do not exist, and the unused vehicle cost counters on journal entries.
  • Romanian translation added.
