Overview

Takes in from the SPV the self-invoices (autofacturi) your customers issue in your name.

Such an invoice is uploaded by the customer, but it carries your company as supplier and the customer as buyer: it is a sale of yours, not a vendor bill. Without this module, the SPV synchronization would import it as a bill.

  • It is recognised from the XML: the supplier is your company and the buyer is somebody else. A document listed as sent counts only when it carries the self-billing type code 389, so your own invoices are never mistaken for one.
  • It becomes a customer invoice. In the customer's self-invoicing journal it keeps the number the customer gave it and is posted; without such a journal it is a draft in the first sale journal.
  • It is marked as validated in the SPV, so it is never sent to ANAF again, and a later synchronization does not duplicate it.
  • Both synchronizations take it in: the l10n_ro_edi one and the SPV messages of l10n_ro_message_spv.

It also covers the other direction, the self-invoices you issue in your suppliers' name (a vendor bill or refund on a Supplier Auto Invoicing journal):

  • they are uploaded to the SPV with autofactura=DA, without which ANAF refuses a document whose supplier is not the uploader, with standard=UBL for invoices and CN only for credit notes, and with ANAF's own refusal reason kept in the chatter;
  • their credit notes carry CreditNoteTypeCode 381, the only one CIUS-RO accepts, instead of the 261 Odoo sets on self-billing credit notes. The invoices already carry 389.

Configuration

For every customer who issues self-invoices in your name, create a sale journal in Accounting → Configuration → Journals with:

Field Value
Type Sales
Ro Sequence Type (l10n_ro_sequence_type) Customer Auto Invoicing
Ro Partner (l10n_ro_partner_id) the customer

For the self-invoices you issue in a supplier's name, the purchase journal works the same way, with Supplier Auto Invoicing and the supplier as Ro Partner.

The fields are in the Ro Auto Invoicing group of the journal form, on sale and purchase journals. The module adds them itself, so l10n_ro_account_enterprise is not needed; when it is installed, its own group shows them instead. Without a journal for the customer, its self-invoices are created as drafts in the first sale journal.

How it works

Nothing to do by hand: the SPV synchronizations take the self-invoices in as they arrive.

  1. The scheduled synchronization of l10n_ro_edi creates them directly as customer invoices.
  2. In SPV Messages they are ticked Self-Invoice; Create Invoice creates them the same way.
  3. A self-invoice posted in the customer's journal carries the customer's number. One left in draft says why in its chatter: no journal for the customer, or the error that stopped the posting.
  4. A self-invoice you issue in a supplier's name is posted on the supplier's Supplier Auto Invoicing purchase journal and sent to ANAF like any invoice (Send & Print or the send cron); it goes up as autofactura.

Versions

19.0.1.1.0 (2026-11-05)

  • A self-invoice taken in from the SPV and left in draft (no self-invoicing journal for the customer) can no longer be deleted, and it can only be posted in a Customer Auto Invoicing journal, where it takes the customer's number; it is never numbered in one of our own sequences.
  • The self-invoicing type and partner of a journal are locked once the journal has posted entries.

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-26)

  • First version: self-invoices issued by customers in the company's name, taken in from the SPV as customer invoices, with the self-invoicing fields on sale and purchase journals; the self-invoices issued in a supplier's name are uploaded with autofactura=DA (moved from account_invoice_delivery_sheet) and their credit notes carry CreditNoteTypeCode 381 (moved from l10n_ro_edi_extension).
