Overview

Self-invoices that your customers issue in your company's name and upload to the SPV are taken in as customer invoices, not as vendor bills. The customer uploads them, but they show your company as supplier: they are your sales.

The module also handles the other direction. When you issue self-invoices in a supplier's name, they are sent to ANAF in the form ANAF accepts.

  • Reliable recognition: a document counts as a self-invoice only when your company is the supplier and the document is marked as self-billed, so your own invoices are never mistaken for one.
  • The customer's number is kept: in a self-invoicing journal set up for that customer, the invoice keeps the number the customer gave it and is posted automatically.
  • No duplicates: the invoice is marked as validated in the SPV, so a later synchronization does not create it again and it is never sent back to ANAF.
  • Both synchronizations covered: the scheduled e-Factura download and the Messages SPV list.
  • Outgoing self-invoices: vendor bills and refunds on a supplier self-invoicing journal are uploaded as self-invoices, and their credit notes carry the type code CIUS-RO accepts.
  • Protected drafts: a self-invoice left in draft cannot be deleted and can only be posted in a customer self-invoicing journal.

Configuration

  1. Go to Invoicing > Configuration > Settings, section Customer Invoices, and check that Create Customer Self-Invoices is enabled for the company.
  2. For every customer who issues self-invoices in your name, open Invoicing > Configuration > Accounting > Journals and create a journal with:
    • Type: Sales;
    • in the Ro Auto Invoicing group, Ro Sequence Type: Customer Auto Invoicing;
    • Ro Partner: the customer.
  3. For the self-invoices you issue in a supplier's name, create a Purchase journal the same way, with Ro Sequence Type Supplier Auto Invoicing and the supplier as Ro Partner.

Without a journal for a customer, its self-invoices are created as drafts in the first sales journal. The self-invoicing type and partner of a journal can no longer be changed once the journal has posted entries.

The SPV connection itself (ANAF credentials) is the one set up for e-Factura.

How it works

Self-invoices received from customers

  1. Nothing to do by hand: the scheduled e-Factura synchronization creates the self-invoices directly as customer invoices.
  2. In Invoicing > Accounting > SPV > Messages SPV they are ticked Self-Invoice; Create Invoice creates them the same way.
  3. Open Invoicing > Customers > Invoices. A self-invoice posted in the customer's journal carries the customer's number.
  4. A self-invoice left in draft explains why in its chatter: no journal for that customer, or the error that stopped the posting. Create the journal, set it on the invoice and confirm.

Self-invoices issued in a supplier's name

  1. Create the vendor bill or refund on the supplier's Supplier Auto Invoicing journal and confirm it.
  2. Send it to ANAF like any invoice, with Send & Print or by the scheduled sending. It is uploaded as a self-invoice, and ANAF's answer is kept in the chatter.
