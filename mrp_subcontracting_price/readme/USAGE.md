1. Send the components to the subcontractor as usual.
2. Go to **Purchase > Orders > Purchase Orders**, create an order for the
   subcontractor and add the finished product at the price the subcontractor
   charges per unit.
3. Confirm the order; the receipt of the finished product is created.
4. Validate the receipt. The finished product enters stock at the purchase
   line price, scaled to the quantity received.
5. Check the result on the journal entry of the receipt: the stock value
   matches the purchase line.
