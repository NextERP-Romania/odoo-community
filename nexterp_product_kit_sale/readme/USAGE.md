1. Go to **Sales > Orders > Quotations** and create a quotation.
2. Add a kit product on **Order Lines** and save.
3. Open the **Kit Order Lines** tab: one line per component, with quantity,
   unit price, taxes and subtotal.
4. Adjust a component's quantity, price or discount if needed; the kit's
   unit price is recalculated.
5. Confirm the order. From now on the component lines can no longer be
   changed.
