Show what a kit is made of on the sales order. The customer sees the kit as
one line, while a **Kit Order Lines** tab lists its components with their own
quantities, prices and taxes.

- Components added automatically when a kit is put on a quotation.
- Each component priced with the order's pricelist, customer, currency and
  fiscal position.
- Edit a component line and the kit price on the order is updated.
- Changing the kit line regenerates its components; deleting it removes
  them.
- Component lines are locked once the order is confirmed or the kit is
  invoiced.
