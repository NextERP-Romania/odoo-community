Show what a kit is made of on the sales order. The customer sees the kit
as one line, while a **Kit Order Lines** tab lists its components with
their own quantities, prices and taxes.

-  Components added automatically when a kit is put on a quotation.
-  Each component priced with the order's pricelist, customer, currency
   and fiscal position.
-  Edit a component line and the kit price on the order is updated.
-  Changing the kit line regenerates its components; deleting it removes
   them.
-  Component lines are locked once the order is confirmed or the kit is
   invoiced.

Configuration
=============

1. Define the kits and their components as described in
   ``nexterp_product_kit``.
2. Make sure the components have the right prices in the pricelists you
   use (**Sales > Products > Pricelists**).
3. Set the taxes on the kit product; they are copied to its components.

Salespeople see the **Kit Order Lines** tab with their usual sales
access.

Usage
=====

1. Go to **Sales > Orders > Quotations** and create a quotation.
2. Add a kit product on **Order Lines** and save.
3. Open the **Kit Order Lines** tab: one line per component, with
   quantity, unit price, taxes and subtotal.
4. Adjust a component's quantity, price or discount if needed; the kit's
   unit price is recalculated.
5. Confirm the order. From now on the component lines can no longer be
   changed.

   kit already billed. Before, deleting them was always allowed.

   quantity any more, since its detail lines would no longer match; the
   description and the other fields stay editable.
-  Deleting a kit detail line no longer risks endless recursion when its
   id matched the id of a sales order line.
