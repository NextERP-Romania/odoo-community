Marks the partners that stand for the companies of your own database, so
transactions between companies of the group can be recognised and filtered
out of reports.

- **Is Inter Company** checkbox on company contacts, ticked automatically on
  the partner of every company of the database.
- Kept up to date when a company is created, removed or linked to another
  partner.
- Can be ticked by hand for group companies managed in another database.
- Base for other modules that flag documents as inter-company, such as
  `nexterp_inter_company_account` for invoices and journal entries.
